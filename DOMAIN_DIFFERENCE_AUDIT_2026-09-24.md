# Civilization Readiness and Domain Difference Audit — 2026-09-24

## Executive verdict

Pausing for a deeper audit was the correct decision.

The intended loop is coherent with the repository:

```text
real-world pressure
  → typed evidence feeds
  → sovereign domain sensing
  → stress + P0–P10 interpretation
  → neurology↔business homology
  → opportunity
  → optional business intervention
  → finance/effect authority
  → receipt + independently observed outcome
  → reafference and learning
```

Most of those nouns already exist in source. They are not yet one trustworthy autonomous loop.
The deployed system is best classified as an **Orchestrator-shaped structural candidate**, not a
conformant autonomous Machine and not yet a self-running civilization. It has many deterministic
and model-assisted lanes, durable records, valves, and observers, but no single zero-token driver
governing every transition, no cryptographically signed receipt chain, no complete runtime chaos
evidence, and no 20-domain production verification.

The most important finding is semantic rather than cosmetic:

- canonical homology transport is present and works;
- production populates it as observational but unestablished across all 20 domains;
- the commercial reflex does not consume that canonical homology context;
- it uses a separate neurological metaphor over stress, novelty, cadence, and selection;
- therefore the present system cannot prove that a commercial intervention is the regulatory
  treatment selected by the neurology↔business mapping that the owner intends.

The safe next move is **not** a rewrite, another master brain, more portal generation, a new phase
ladder, or broader live effect authority. It is a narrow semantic commissioning slice: establish
and enforce one canonical homology-to-intervention contract in one reversible lane, prove it by
replay and independent observation, and only then replicate it.

No runtime, protected file, portal, domain brain, money rail, provider account, deployment, or
production control was changed during this audit. CalcStack expansion, mortgage/credit work,
portal regeneration, Tradier production work, and new autonomous businesses remain held.

preview, approval, last-moment adapter guard, receipt, reconcile, and cancel stages. There is no
production brokerage selector. Keep Tradier on the back burner until common semantics, atomic

   let both continue as equivalent truth.

   bidirectional mapping from admitted evidence.
   Reuse the existing `limen-worker-snapshot` domain-company join or a typed shared store; do not

   datasets and authenticated read models into the hosted runner. Do not expose general `fetch`,
   POST routes, provider adapters, or request-derived origins to the VM.

   verifier scope.

2. Reconcile heartbeat and civilization-valve semantics; an unreadable outward control must not
   appear open.

For each lane, prove exact work item → decision → authorization → effect receipt → independent
outcome → learning/recovery. Exercise duplicate delivery, crash/resume, stale verifier, budget
exhaustion, quarantine, rollback, and control-store failure in a non-production or bounded sandbox.
Only then mark that lane production verified.

1. Add atomic capital reservation across concurrent receipts.
2. Reconcile external balances before granting spend authority.

1. Treat CalcStack as a typed evidence and traffic source, not a diagnosis engine.
2. Build mortgage then credit as real-world Finance surfaces after data contracts are ratified.
3. Finish the four domain embed gaps without changing domain cognition.
4. Resume portal generation only from stable identity, provenance, and quality gates.

### R6 — expand optional businesses and, much later, physical agents

Businesses may be created, invested in, marketed, or declined according to the established
regulatory need and Finance policy. Killswitch Websites and Relay can become reference patterns
only after their common contracts—not their bespoke internals—are extracted and verified.

AGI/model-training ambitions and drones remain later programs. A general model may learn from
verified traces; it must not become an unverified master action selector. Drones require separate
identity, geofencing, command authentication, human authorization, fail-safe landing, and physical
safety evidence.

## Final audit disposition

The project is moving in the right conceptual direction, and the owner is not drifting. The code
base already contains enough components that a rewrite would be counterproductive. The risk is
that similarly named layers are being mistaken for one connected mechanism:

```text
live sensing ≠ observation freshness
stress ≠ phase
phase affinity ≠ current phase
opportunity ≠ established homology
neurological labels ≠ canonical neurology↔business evidence
planned intent ≠ authorized effect
durable receipt ≠ independently verified outcome
structural implementation ≠ production proof
```

The civilization becomes plausible by making those boundaries explicit and closing one measured
loop at a time. The first target is canonical homology admission in one reversible lane, followed
by common control/cost truth—not more surface area.
