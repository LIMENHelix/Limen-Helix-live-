Most organizations are structurally lesioned.

They acquire without removing. They grow without maintenance. They add excitation without matched inhibition. Then they accumulate debt, lose flexibility, and fail the way damaged brains fail.

LIMEN Helix maps neural dynamics onto business and civilization systems using structural homology, not metaphor. It builds the inhibition, extinction and clearance mechanisms in from the start, so an organization’s failure modes are visible and recoverable.

Reading the instruments…
Thalamic gating: Ferrarelli & Tononi, Schizophrenia Bulletin, 2010 — what reaches attention, and what is filtered out before it does.

The same named failure mode, in neural tissue and in a power grid, with the regulatory partner identified and its presence or absence stated. That is what “homology, not metaphor” has to mean to be worth anything.

What it does
  • Detects and names pathology. Gating failures, disinhibition, oscillatory pathology, seizure-like runaway, incomplete circuits. There is no autonomous correction: a human reads the diagnosis and decides.
  • Routes decisions through a hierarchy that preserves the brain’s feedforward and feedback lamination, and thalamic gating for filtering. Prevents both flooding and starvation.
  • Learns only from verified outcomes, not self-report. Its validated scorer returns nothing at all rather than a plausible number it cannot stand behind. Silence instead of noise.
  • Scales time constants across neural, business and civilization while preserving the parameter ratios — the fractal invariant.
  • Twenty domain units feeding one integration layer over an inter-brain bus, with cron-driven consolidation. Each domain is a brain, not a silo.
What it is being built to do
  • Transfer cross-domain integration from the operator into the substrate. Today a human still holds that loop and reweights by hand when one domain contradicts another. Moving it into the system is the long goal, and it is not done.
  • Consolidate offline. The cycles run, in shadow and observe-only.
  • Execute gated actions. Action selection is dark-armed and stays that way until the loop is proven on a single domain against ground truth.
Current state

Live now: twenty domain units running; cron-driven consolidation firing on schedule; deployed with durable persistence; the validated scorer returning verified outcomes only.

Not finished, and said plainly: partially built. The integration transfer is staged, not delivered. Execution gates remain observe-only by design rather than by accident.
