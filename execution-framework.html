LIMEN HELIX EXECUTION FRAMEWORK

This document defines the compensation, responsibilities, and rules for all execution paths in the LIMEN system. Every operator and auditor must understand these terms before claiming opportunities.

This framework is the authoritative source for all operator compensation and execution rules. Individual opportunity cards display a summary. This page contains the full terms.

COMPENSATION MODELS

Grant / Deal Execution

Operators who successfully secure grant funding or close deals on behalf of the organization.

TierCompensationRequirement
Tier 1 (Start)10% of grant award or deal valueDefault for all new operators
Tier 215%3 successful grant awards or deals closed
Tier 325%10 successful outcomes

Investment Execution

Operators who execute investment positions based on system-generated opportunities.

TierCompensationRequirement
Tier 1 (Start)5% of realized profitDefault
Tier 210% of realized profit3 profitable positions closed
Tier 315% of realized profit10 profitable positions

Note: Compensation is on realized profit only. No compensation on losses. Paper trading does not count toward tier advancement.

Patent Execution

Operators who identify, draft, and file patent applications based on system-identified gaps.

TierCompensationRequirement
Tier 1 (Start)10% of licensing royaltiesDefault
Tier 215% of licensing royalties3 patents filed

Tier 330-35% of net profit + priority access to new opportunitiesSustained profitable operations across portfolio

PAYOUT RULES

Timing

Grant: Paid upon disbursement of grant funds to the organization.

Invest: Paid upon close of profitable position (realized gains only).

Patent: Paid quarterly on collected licensing revenue.

Business: Paid monthly from net operating profit after month 3 (first 3 months retained for working capital).

Loss Handling

Investment losses are not charged to the operator. However, repeated losses (3+ consecutive) may result in tier review. The organization absorbs capital risk. Operators absorb execution time risk.

TIER ADVANCEMENT

Tiers are tracked per execution type. An operator at Tier 3 for Grants may be Tier 1 for Patents. Tier advancement is based on successful outcomes, not activity volume.

Tier review occurs quarterly. Downgrade only for sustained non-performance (6+ months no activity) or compliance violations.

OPERATOR RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Execute opportunities claimed from the system using the provided workspace tools.

2. Follow step-by-step execution instructions. Do not skip steps.

3. Submit completed work for auditor review before final execution.

4. Report outcomes honestly. Do not fabricate results.

5. Maintain document completeness for all active workspaces.

AUDITOR RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Review submitted workspaces for completeness and accuracy.

2. Approve, deny, or request revisions with structured reasoning.

3. Validate financial assumptions and projections.

4. Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

5. Track operator tier status and advancement eligibility.

DEFINITIONS

Opportunity: A system-identified action path generated from live domain diagnosis.

Claim: Operator acceptance of responsibility for executing an opportunity.

Workspace: The execution environment for a specific opportunity type (Grant, Patent, Business, Invest).

Outcome: A measurable result from execution (grant awarded, patent filed, business launched, position closed).

Revalidation: Periodic check that the opportunity still has supporting evidence from live data.

DISPUTE HANDLING

Disputes regarding compensation, tier status, or outcome classification are reviewed by the auditor layer. If unresolved, escalation follows the organizational dispute resolution process. No unilateral changes to compensation terms after claim acceptance.

By claiming an opportunity, the operator agrees to the terms in this framework. Each opportunity card includes a summary. This page is the full reference.
