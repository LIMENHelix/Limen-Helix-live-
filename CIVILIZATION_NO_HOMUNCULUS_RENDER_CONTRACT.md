# Civilization No-Homunculus Architecture and Render Contract

**Status:** owner-authoritative architectural constraint
**Recorded:** 2026-08-27
**Applies to:** `/civilization`, related diagrams, operator surfaces, bridge code, and architecture language

This record captures the approved architecture decision. It does **not** claim that a current or revised render has passed visual inspection. A render passes only after it is inspected against every criterion below.

## Causal topology

1. **No processing node exists above the connectome.** Synthesis, foresight, prioritization, diagnosis, and learning are properties of the reciprocal collective state. They may be annotated on the connectome but must not be drawn or implemented as a downstream tier that receives the connectome's output and governs the sovereign domain brains.
2. **Domains and connectome influence one another.** The causal path is reciprocal: domains emit typed evidence into the connectome, and bounded connectome signals modulate domains through explicit descending paths. Every path preserves provenance, domain ownership, confidence, authority, and inhibition.
3. **Memory is a lateral substrate.** Domain brains and the connectome continuously read and write authorized durable memory. Memory is not a terminal output bucket below cognition and does not become a central decision-maker.
4. **The observer is a readout tap.** The Civilization Briefing and related operator dashboards may measure and summarize connectome state, but they are not stages in the causal chain and have no implicit authority to select or execute actions.

## Sovereignty and action

- Each domain brain selects, inhibits, executes, observes, and learns within its own explicit authority and resource envelope.
- Cross-domain coordination occurs through typed connectome packets and separately authorized economic transfers, not through a hidden master controller.
- Any outward effect remains attributable to the sovereign domain, lane, valve, decision record, receipt, and outcome observer that produced it.
- No interface label such as “Master Brain,” “Civilization AI,” “consciousness,” or “briefing” grants causal authority.

## Render acceptance test

A public or administrative diagram passes only when visual inspection confirms all four conditions:

- no node or processing tier above the connectome;
- visible arrows in both directions between domains and connectome;
- memory beside and coupled to the recurrent loop;
- observer/briefing drawn off to the side as a readout tap.

Removing a disproven master-controller tier is an architectural correction, not merely a cosmetic rename. Obsolete structure must be excluded by construction rather than left dormant or visually implied.

## Reconciliation rule

This contract supersedes older project language that describes a “Master Brain” as a superordinate evaluator, selector, executor, or delegator. Historical filenames may remain until safely migrated, but they must be interpreted as compatibility labels and cannot establish a causal tier or authority.
