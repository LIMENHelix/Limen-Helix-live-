How it works What is LIMEN Helix?

What is LIMEN Helix?

LIMEN means threshold — the exact moment a thing tips from one state into another. Helix is the spiral it turns in. Put together: we watch for the thresholds where things change — a home sliding toward foreclosure, a company toward failure, a neighborhood toward a boom — and we get there before everyone else.

It isn't an app you run or a report you read. Think of it as a scout that watches every major market around the clock and hands you the opening while it's still open.
