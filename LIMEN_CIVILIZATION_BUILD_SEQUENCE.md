# LIMEN Civilization Build Sequence

**Status:** authoritative execution plan  
**Scope:** conceptual P0–P10 phase language, neurology↔business homology, brain-v2, Civilization/Master Brain wiring, research/investment autonomy, and later domain expansion  
**Created:** 2026-08-23  

This document is the leading source for sequencing the work. Jobs are completed in order. A later job may not silently bypass an earlier gate.

## Operating rule

P0–P10 and the neurology↔business↔civilization mapping are the system's conceptual design language. They are not treated as prerequisites for proving a scientific civilization-scale theory. We use them to organize the build and to preserve intended functional distinctions.

Evidence gates apply to a different layer: software behavior, source provenance, persistence, action receipts, outcome resolution, financial controls, and user-visible utility. Do not block implementation waiting for neuroscience validation; do not describe conceptual mappings as established scientific findings.
