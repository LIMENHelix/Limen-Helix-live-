# LIMEN Civilization Build Sequence

**Status:** authoritative execution plan  
**Scope:** conceptual P0–P10 phase language, neurology↔business homology, brain-v2, Civilization/Master Brain wiring, research/investment autonomy, and later domain expansion  
**Created:** 2026-08-23  

This document is the leading source for sequencing the work. Jobs are completed in order. A later job may not silently bypass an earlier gate.

## Operating rule

P0–P10 and the neurology↔business↔civilization mapping are the system's conceptual design language. They are not treated as prerequisites for proving a scientific civilization-scale theory. We use them to organize the build and to preserve intended functional distinctions.

Evidence gates apply to a different layer: software behavior, source provenance, persistence, action receipts, outcome resolution, financial controls, and user-visible utility. Do not block implementation waiting for neuroscience validation; do not describe conceptual mappings as established scientific findings.

## Job 1 — Freeze the conceptual P0–P10 registry

**Target window:** 1–2 weeks  
**Owner:** brain-v2/core + documentation  
**Change type:** design/schema first; no money movement

### Work

- Reconcile phase names across Git and Drive.
- Resolve repository-specific P7a/P7b variants.
- Preserve the conceptual sequence: Source, Rupture, Rhythm, Darkness, Peace, Awareness, Order, Dissolution, Witness, Threshold, Resurrection unless an explicit owner decision changes it.
- Store phase labels, descriptions, transitions, and aliases in one versioned registry.
- Mark phase content as conceptual, hypothesis, mixed, or interpretive where appropriate.

### Gate

- One registry is consumed by the renderer and brain-side code.
- No unresolved aliases or conflicting labels remain.
- Phase status cannot silently upgrade a concept into a measured claim.

## Job 2 — Build the neural → business → civilization homology ledger

**Target window:** 2–4 weeks  
**Owner:** brain-v2 design layer  
**Change type:** conceptual map with explicit assumptions

### Work

For each B0–B17 mechanism, record:

- neural function/topology
- business equivalent
- civilization equivalent
- shared failure mode
- shared intervention/control class
- intended invariant
- implementation location
- assumption or boundary note

Use the three-part homology test: shared topology, shared failure mode, and shared intervention class. Rows may remain conceptual; the ledger must say so.

### Gate

- Every mapping is classified as conceptual, structurally supported, measured in software, or unresolved.
- No analogy is used as a runtime safety claim without an engineering test.

## Job 3 — Repair the sensory substrate across all 20 domains

**Target window:** 3–6 weeks  
**Owner:** ingestion, recorder, brain-v2 adapters  
**Change type:** runtime/data

### Work

- Populate or explicitly abstain every domain queue.
- Complete corpus-to-domain ingestion, including the agriculture P2/deep-file gap.
- Preserve semantic title/evidence content with source identity and provenance.
- Reconcile units, cadence, revisions, timestamps, and fallback behavior.
- Measure replayability and source freshness for every domain.
- Keep unsupported or unavailable providers abstaining instead of filling gaps with saturated constants.

### Gate

- Every domain has measurable input, provenance, freshness, replay behavior, and an explicit abstention state.
- No article count, missing API value, or fallback constant directly creates stress or activation.

## Job 4 — Create the explicit Civilization ↔ Master Brain bridge

**Target window:** 3–5 weeks  
**Owner:** domain-brain adapter, Civilization, Master Brain  
**Change type:** runtime contract

### Work

Replace implicit shared-global assumptions with a versioned handoff carrying:

- domain identity, cycle number, and timing
- feeds, freshness, and source health
- diagnoses and near-diagnoses
- opportunities and treatments
- phase vector, trajectory, stress, and confidence
- cross-domain emissions and incoming signals
- company bindings
- directives with citations, monitoring, and escalation
- maturity and evidence status

### Gate

- One integration test proves the same evidence survives from domain input to Master Brain output.
- Stale reports, missing fields, and race conditions are surfaced rather than silently merged.

## Job 5 — Close the brain loops

**Target window:** 6–10 weeks  
**Owner:** brain-v2 kernel and actuators  
**Change type:** runtime behavior

Execute in this order:

1. **B11 + B14:** command, efference copy, reafference, and trusted self-effect attribution.
2. **B9:** descending and lateral cross-domain pathways.
3. **B13:** durable offline consolidation with retirement authority.
4. **B17:** self-model, observability inventory, and metaplasticity.

### Gate

Each loop must have runtime evidence, independent outcome resolution, persistence, and a negative/failure test. Wiring alone does not count as closure.

## Job 6 — Run the full read-only civilization sandbox

**Target window:** 2–4 weeks after Jobs 3–5  
**Owner:** sandbox/rehearsal harness  
**Change type:** simulation only

Run a 30-day rehearsal across all domains and lanes. Record every proposed action, simulated result, cost, provenance, persistence result, and blocker. Lanes remain simulated: research, investment, publication, social, subscriber/email, CRM, real estate/relay, auction, homestead, and broker/order.

### Gate

- No lane is unclassified.
- No simulated action is reported as executed.
- No missing receipt, unexplained spend, persistence failure, or hidden manual intervention remains.

## Job 7 — Paper-operation pilot

**Target window:** 1–3 months  
**Owner:** research and finance lanes  
**Change type:** paper operation

- Research: science and medicine evidence production.
- Investment: finance opportunities, paper brokerage, and simulated cash ledger.
- Preserve research provenance and investment rationale.
- Score outcomes independently from the originating signal.

### Gate

Research outputs are useful and traceable; investment decisions are reproducible, bounded, receipted, and resolved against later observations.

## Job 8 — Small real-money finance pilot

**Target window:** 3–6 months after Job 7  
**Owner:** finance lane with owner authorization  
**Change type:** controlled production money movement

Requirements:

- cash account only; no leverage
- very small capital limit
- daily and position limits
- broker-side controls
- human kill switch and override
- order/fill/balance reconciliation
- independent outcome ledger

### Gate

No unreconciled order, unexplained action, missing receipt, or unavailable kill switch during the defined observation period.

## Job 9 — Expand the civilization one lane at a time

**Target window:** 12–24+ months  
**Owner:** per-lane owners

Only after research and finance are reliable, add revenue/publication flows, company/vendor operations, relay real estate, social/subscriber systems, and inter-domain banking. Each lane receives its own evidence, provenance, rollback, and readiness gate.
