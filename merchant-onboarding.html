Frequently Asked Questions

What's the commission structure?

For every sale, Relay takes 20% (15% marketplace fee + 5% franchise fee). You keep 80%. For a $50 item, you earn $40.

When do I get paid?

Payouts are processed weekly. Pending earnings show in your dashboard in real-time. Transfers go to your connected bank account.

Can I sell unlimited items?

Yes. There's no limit on inventory. List as many items as you want. You only pay commission on completed sales.

How do buyers find my items?

All Relay merchants' items appear on our shared storefront at /marketplace-storefront. Buyers browse by category, condition, and price.

Can I sync from other marketplaces?

Yes. Use our CSV importer or marketplace scraper to pull items from Vinted, Poshmark, Mercari, etc. Images download automatically.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Relay is free to join. There are no monthly fees, listing fees, or upfront costs. You only pay commission on sales.

What payment methods do buyers use?

Buyers pay via credit card through Stripe Checkout. Payments are secure and encrypted.

Can I manage multiple stores?

Each seller account is independent. Create multiple accounts if you want separate storefronts or business models.
