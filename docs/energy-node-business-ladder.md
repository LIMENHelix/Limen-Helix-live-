## Per-rung: the four relations

### L1 — Distressed-energy-asset broker (NO CAPITAL)

**Dysregulation → regulation (treatment).** The energy dysregulation is *stranded / retiring assets*:
a fossil plant is deactivating, an operator is in distress, capital is trapped in a dying asset. The
regulation is not to save the plant; it is to **clear the defunct asset and repurpose its substrate**
for new use. The broker detects the dying asset and routes it to whoever will repurpose it.

**Node → business (biology informs business).** Microglia are the brain's resident immune
surveillance: they patrol tissue, detect damaged/dying neurons, and phagocytose them — but critically,
microglial + associated pruning **clears the defunct while freeing and preserving the substrate**
(space, scaffolding, connection sites) for new growth. Action = surveil → detect distress → clear →
free the reusable substrate. That is exactly the broker: surveil the sector → detect the dying plant →
"clear" it to a buyer → and the reusable substrate that carries the value is the plant's existing
**grid interconnection** (years of queue time already paid for). Same operation, same mechanism.

**Business → node (prompts research).** The broker's insight is that the *dead* asset's value lives in
its **retained interconnection scaffolding**, not the asset itself. Research question (hypothesis, NOT
lit-checked, do not claim novel until verified): does microglial clearance run a *connection-preserving*
mode that selectively spares synaptic scaffolding / axon-guidance paths of a pruned neuron so the
circuit can rewire faster into the freed slot — analogous to preserving the grid interconnection of a
retired plant for a repowering developer? If business monetizes "preserve the interconnect, discard the
asset," is there a matching preserve-the-connection-discard-the-cell pruning program neuroscience has
not framed this way?

**L1 no-capital revenue strategy (in depth).** See the dedicated section below.

### L2 — Energy-bill / usage X-ray (MINIMAL)

- **Dysregulation → regulation:** a facility/household is *interoceptively blind* to its own energy
  waste (can't feel its internal state). Regulation = give it a sense organ: read consumption, flag
  deviation from a benchmark set-point, surface the waste.
- **Node → business:** the insula maps the body's internal physiological state into awareness and
  flags deviation (too hot, too hungry, heart racing). The bill X-ray maps a site's internal energy
  state into awareness and flags deviation from benchmark. Same operation: interoceptive read + deviation
  flag. (Free tier = the sense organ; paid = the deeper diagnostic.)
- **Business → node (research):** the product tunes the "normal" set-point per building type. Question:
  is interoceptive set-point learned per-organ from that organ's own history the way per-building
  benchmarks are, and does mis-set interoceptive baseline drive somatic-symptom disorders the way a
  wrong benchmark drives false waste alarms? (hypothesis, needs lit-check.)

### L3 — Grid/asset condition-monitoring software (SMALL)

- **Dysregulation → regulation:** transmission congestion / voltage instability / line overload =
  a signal-routing system overwhelmed. Regulation = relay the telemetry and gate anomalies to the
  right operator before overload.
- **Node → business:** the thalamus relays nearly all signal traffic and its reticular nucleus applies
  inhibitory gain-control, passing salient signals and suppressing noise. Grid SCADA / condition-
  monitoring relays sensor telemetry and gates anomalies. Same operation: relay + salience-gate.
  (Directory: "Grid automation and SCADA vendor", confidence 0.90.)
- **Business → node (research):** predictive-alert software learns which telemetry patterns precede
  overload. Question: does the reticular nucleus's gating threshold adapt from *near-miss* history
  (pre-overload signatures) the way the software does? (hypothesis, needs lit-check.)

### L4 — Predictive-maintenance / O&M field service (MODERATE)

- **Dysregulation → regulation:** generator failure / line degradation = drift from intended operating
  state. Regulation = detect the drift early and correct it before failure.
- **Node → business:** the cerebellum runs **forward models** — it predicts the sensory consequence of
  an action and issues error-corrections when reality drifts from prediction. Predictive maintenance
  forecasts failure from drift signatures and dispatches a correction. Same operation: forward-model +
  error-correct. (Directory: O&M contractor 0.80, line contractor 0.82.)
- **Business → node (research):** the best drift-signature math in predictive maintenance is a specific
  forward-model form. Question: does that form describe a cerebellar-timing model the physiological-
  failure-prediction literature has not tested? (hypothesis, needs lit-check.)

### L5 — Equipment manufacturing / renewable EPC (LARGE) — *moderate confidence*

- **Dysregulation → regulation:** equipment supply gap. Regulation = produce the hardware that actuates
  generation/T&D.
- **Node → business:** motor cortex is the final cortical output stage that produces actuation.
  Manufacturing/EPC produces the physical actuation hardware. Match is by *role* (produce the output),
  weaker on mechanism — flag as analogy, not strict isomorphism.

### L6 — Own generation / storage (INVESTMENT) — *terminal*

- **Dysregulation → regulation:** capacity shortfall itself. Regulation = be the organ that produces
  the output (own the plant/storage).
- **Node → business:** at this rung the node *is* the business — M1 = power generation, HIPP = battery
  storage, VTA = solar/wind, PAG = nuclear (directory's top-tier, 0.95). Heavy capital / project finance.

## L1 in depth: the no-capital revenue engine

**Why it produces revenue with zero capital.** You own nothing. You detect a distressed / retiring
energy asset from FREE public signals, and you connect it to a party who pays to acquire or repurpose
it. Revenue = a finder / brokerage fee on the connection, or selling the distress feed as data. No
inventory, no plant, no balance sheet. This reuses the P3 spine already live for Medicine/Technology/
Finance (signal → rank → counterparty registry → desk → cron).

**The signal (free, public):**
- **Generator deactivation / retirement filings** — ISO/RTO deactivation requests (PJM, MISO, etc.
  publish these), EIA-860 generator retirements, utility IRPs, FERC filings.
- **Energy-sector corporate distress** — SEC EDGAR 8-K bankruptcy/default for public energy companies
  (already wired in the Finance instance; filter to energy SIC).
- **Facility closures** — WARN notices at energy/industrial sites.

**The reusable-substrate insight (the moat).** A retiring fossil plant carries an existing **grid
interconnection** — often years of queue time and millions in study costs already sunk. To a repowering
developer (battery, solar), that interconnection is the prize, worth far more than the scrap. The broker's
edge is spotting "dying asset WITH a valuable live interconnection" and routing it before the queue-
watchers do. This is the microglial "preserve the connection, discard the cell" move, monetized.

**The counterparty (who pays):**
- Repowering developers (battery/solar seeking ready interconnection),
- Distressed-energy PE / infrastructure funds,
- Decommissioning / scrap contractors,
- Site/land buyers.

**Capital-light execution:** detect (free feeds) → rank (interconnection value + distress severity) →
match to a counterparty registry → hand to a licensed closer. Same code shape as the other P3 desks.
Honest register: LAGGING public signal, no validated kernel → rank 1-3, "find and connect fast", never
"predict".
