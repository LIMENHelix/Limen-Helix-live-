# LIMEN Helix — Autonomy Continuity Goal

**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Purpose:** Keep the implementation focused while the LIMEN Helix system is made genuinely observable, domain-sovereign, economically closed-loop, and safe to continue improving.

## The single objective

Build and prove the smallest honest end-to-end autonomy spine for every sovereign domain:

```text
source/feed → domain brain → diagnosis → typed opportunity → owning business motor
→ decision → command → provider/owned receipt → independent outcome observation
→ revenue or non-revenue result → returned afferent → learning → next evaluation
```

The operator must be able to see that chain in the UI. A brain producing a diagnosis or opportunity is not the same thing as a business motor completing work, and a provider receipt is not the same thing as revenue.

## Current focus

Continue from the repaired 20-domain brain and business wiring. The next implementation surface is the spider-web propagation path edge to edge: domain state, cross-domain routing, owned business consumer, result persistence, returned observation, learning, and visible operator status.

The first missing load-bearing transition is repaired one domain at a time. Continue automatically through the next safe domain while tests, provenance, ownership, and fail-closed gates remain intact.

## Non-negotiable architecture

### Brain and business are separate layers

- A domain brain senses, interprets, diagnoses, regulates, and emits typed opportunities.
- A business motor is an owning-domain consumer of a typed opportunity.
- The motor has its own decision, command, receipt, observer, rollback, budget, and learning contracts.
- Business readiness must never be inferred from brain activity alone.
- Business evidence must never rewrite the domain brain's neurology or transfer ownership.

### Domain ownership and routing

- Each of the 20 domains remains sovereign.
- Agriculture investment opportunities route to Finance.
- Agriculture research opportunities route to Science/research.
- Homestead is a separate property-availability lookup/publication surface. It is not Agriculture's stress loop and must not be used as Agriculture end-to-end execution evidence.
- If an older repository document describes an Agriculture Homestead/Farm Operations motor, treat that as historical or conflicting evidence. The current owner clarification controls this goal: do not restore that Agriculture execution lane.
- No domain may borrow another domain's cognition, motor receipt, provider identity, or authority.

### P0–P10 fidelity

- Keep phase and business capital tier as separate axes.
- P0–P10 is a cycle grammar, not an eleven-step distress scale.
- Dysregulation/stress is a modifier of transition and operating posture, not an invented phase.
- Use coverage—not raw funds—as the economic stress sensor: revenue generated at a tier divided by the capital required by the next tier.
- Prefer the shared capital-pool model over a brittle serial bucket brigade.
- Preserve function-before-structure failure handling.
- Thresholds in the P0–P10 analysis are placeholders until explicitly committed and backtested; do not turn them into runtime authority by implication.

## Definition of done

This goal is complete only when:

- Every sovereign domain has an explicit, tested path or an exact named blocker at the first safe boundary.
- Brain output and business-motor output are separately visible and separately authoritative.
- Investment and research routing is explicit, including Agriculture → Finance and Agriculture → Science/research.
- Homestead remains a separate property catalog/publication path.
- Every claimed motor has durable decision, command, receipt, observer, and learning evidence, or is visibly held/unobserved.
- Revenue generation is pinned to the owning domain and is never inferred from opportunity count, page existence, checkout intent, or a provider receipt alone.
- Cross-domain propagation is traceable in both directions: emission/routing outward and observed outcome/learning inward.
- UI surfaces reveal freshness, state, blockers, and result location without implying unsupported autonomy.
- The route/link inventory is reconciled into live, broken, duplicate, legacy, data-only, operator, and customer categories.
- No unresolved drift, duplicate architecture, ownership conflict, fabricated evidence, silent failure, or test regression remains hidden.

## Drift and safety rules

Continue without pausing for ordinary bounded implementation, testing, documentation, and commits when the current task authorizes them and the checks remain green. Stop and record a blocker immediately when any of the following occurs:

- A source document conflicts with an owner decision or current code contract.
- A proposed change would reinterpret P0–P10, change neurology, merge brain and business authority, or transfer domain ownership.
- A provider credential, account value, legal/professional authority, owner decision, or external-action authorization is required.
- The only way to make a test pass is to fabricate a receipt, weaken a gate, hide a failed observation, or relabel a fixture as production evidence.
- A deployment, trade, spend, email, publication, incorporation, fund transfer, or other consequential action would be required.
- The same defect cannot be explained by evidence after two bounded repair attempts.

When stopped, state the exact boundary, evidence, safe next action, and what remains unclaimed. Do not silently broaden the goal.
