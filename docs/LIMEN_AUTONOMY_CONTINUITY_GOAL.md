## The single objective

Build and prove the smallest honest end-to-end autonomy spine for every sovereign domain:

```text
source/feed → domain brain → diagnosis → typed opportunity → owning business motor
→ decision → command → provider/owned receipt → independent outcome observation
→ revenue or non-revenue result → returned afferent → learning → next evaluation
```

The operator must be able to see that chain in the UI. A brain producing a diagnosis or opportunity is not the same thing as a business motor completing work, and a provider receipt is not the same thing as revenue.

## Non-negotiable architecture

### Brain and business are separate layers

- A domain brain senses, interprets, diagnoses, regulates, and emits typed opportunities.
- A business motor is an owning-domain consumer of a typed opportunity.
- The motor has its own decision, command, receipt, observer, rollback, budget, and learning contracts.
- Business readiness must never be inferred from brain activity alone.
- Business evidence must never rewrite the domain brain's neurology or transfer ownership.

### Domain ownership and routing

- Each of the 20 domains remains sovereign.
- Agriculture investment opportunities route to Finance.
- Agriculture research opportunities route to Science/research.
- Homestead is a separate property-availability lookup/publication surface. It is not Agriculture's stress loop and must not be used as Agriculture end-to-end execution evidence.
- If an older repository document describes an Agriculture Homestead/Farm Operations motor, treat that as historical or conflicting evidence. The current owner clarification controls this goal: do not restore that Agriculture execution lane.
- No domain may borrow another domain's cognition, motor receipt, provider identity, or authority.

### P0–P10 fidelity

- Keep phase and business capital tier as separate axes.
- P0–P10 is a cycle grammar, not an eleven-step distress scale.
- Dysregulation/stress is a modifier of transition and operating posture, not an invented phase.
- Use coverage—not raw funds—as the economic stress sensor: revenue generated at a tier divided by the capital required by the next tier.
- Prefer the shared capital-pool model over a brittle serial bucket brigade.
- Preserve function-before-structure failure handling.
- Thresholds in the P0–P10 analysis are placeholders until explicitly committed and backtested; do not turn them into runtime authority by implication.
