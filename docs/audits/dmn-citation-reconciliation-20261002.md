# DMN citation reconciliation — current experimental scope

54 reference entries in this expansion, including partial-access and unresolved/candidate entries. Earlier targeted corrections retained in phase-reconciliation-current-20261002.md. Not a completed every-entry/full-text bibliography review or validation of LIMEN phase mappings. Experimental framework retained per D-028/D-029.

The original Word source remains unchanged. Bibliographic defects and measured-claim attribution are reconciled individually. Review articles, theories, animal experiments, subjective questionnaires and direct human neuroimaging are identified separately; these sources can motivate LIMEN hypotheses without establishing the proposed phase assignment. Missing independent validation does not block experimental engineering.

Specific additional corrections: Lebedev2015 third author is Gidon Rosenthal, not Roesttorp. Lanius2010 title is truncated. Carhart-Harris2017 bibliography omits its final author David J Nutt. Floatation and drumming papers measure reported affect or endocrine/immune outcomes rather than DMN activity. Flow has regional increases as well as decreases. Thalamic GABA in the yoga experiment is not an ACC-coherence or ventral-vagal measurement. Nour2016 validates a subjective inventory, without neuroimaging.

The two table-only entries Meeusen/Roelands2018 and Tagliazucchi2016 retain candidate identities; no silent substitution is made. Exact Szabo2014 remains unresolved. Brewer2011, Muzik2018, Castellanos2008 and Sheline2009 have partial-result access in this expansion; an identity match is not a full claim-result confirmation. Foundational and critical-transition papers remain useful. The earlier Daniels/Uddin/Voon/Hamilton/Young/Borjigin/Dixon/Newberg/Fox findings remain in the existing reconciliation checkpoint.

## Phase wording ready for review

P1: “Light / first distinction — collapse of prior symmetry into a localized recursive core.” This follows the existing ARC equation `R(Σ) → R(L), L ⊂ Σ`; it preserves collapse as a symmetry mechanism and separates it from bankruptcy. No label or frozen detector was changed.

P7: preserve separation and the two-outcome decision mechanism. Use “P7 divergence fork” for the whole node in a reconciled explanatory map, and retain P7a/P7b for implemented branch readings. This is a proposed terminology bridge, not ratification: ARC calls the whole node P7b and describes re-entry toward P8, whereas the registry assigns P7b to recovery toward P10. Existing scores are revisable and decaying, not enforced absorbing states. Endpoint policy remains unresolved.

Timing: “coincident (~1q lead), not ahead” is internally inconsistent. Actual lead must be traced to dated observations/breaks before publishing a replacement timing claim. The available 24/30 chart annotation remains inadequately derived from stored run30b records; preserve historical artifacts.
