## What you're selling (and why it's an easy open)
You're **not** cold-pushing a product nobody asked for. You walk into small and new clinics — urgent care, dental, med-spa, primary care, vet, physical therapy — and hand them something **free and genuinely useful**: instant alerts whenever the FDA recalls a drug or device, so a recall never slips past their practice and turns into a lawsuit.

The free alerts get you in the door. Once a clinic sees how much liability we quietly take off their plate, most upgrade to the paid **Safety Bundle** (tuned monitoring, instant Class I alerts, and a compliance log for malpractice defense). That upgrade is your commission.

Think of it like the "free website" pitch — you lead with a gift, and the paid stuff sells itself once they're using it.

## Your day
- Build a list of small/new clinics in your area (we show you how, and give you a starter list).
- Drop in, call, or email: "We send clinics free FDA recall alerts — want yours turned on?"
- Get them onto the free digest (2-minute signup).
- Follow up, show the paid protection, close the bundle.
- We handle the product, the alerts, and the tech. You handle relationships.
