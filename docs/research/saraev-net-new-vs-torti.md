## B. Anti-catalog — Saraev's calibration WARNING (saves us from building losers)
From "Most Overrated AI Agents": **never put AI at the first human point of contact** — receptionist / appointment-setter / closer / outbound-voice / front-line support DESTROY conversion (human setter ~30% -> AI ~20%; on 100 leads x $1k you save $3k on staff but lose $7k). This directly CALIBRATES Torti's flagship "inbound receptionist" and "support agent" offers: scope them as procedural back-ends (Amazon-style refund equation) or human-in-the-loop only. Also DON'T sell: basic FAQ chatbots, AI SEO content (window closed 2022), full-autonomy email responders, giant all-in-one n8n agents (1-5% failure != 1% revenue loss). **Doctrine:** every build must emit a TANGIBLE deliverable (doc/PDF/dashboard/notification), and take a working revenue pipeline and make it a little better rather than build net-new.
