## 4. Torti's pricing + sales mechanics (bolt onto deal engine / sales machine)
- **Value-based pricing:** cost-saving = hours-saved/wk × rate × 52 × 20-25%; revenue-uplift = added annual revenue × 10%; retainer = 20% of setup. Cautionary tale: charged $500 for a system that made the client $8k.
- **"Sell the outcome, don't say AI."** "We're not selling a voice agent." Confused buyer never buys.
- **Niche 5-point filter:** LTV >$3k · obvious daily pain · reach the owner · ROI <30 days · no heavy compliance.
- **7 businesses to AVOID:** franchises (no tech control), no-digital-foundation, free-tool-already-solves-it, volume-too-low, pre-revenue, regulated/data-processor, vertical-SaaS-already-shipping-AI.
- **Sales spine:** send offer doc pre-call → let them state the problem → amplify pain + future-pace cost of inaction → ask permission to pitch → present a NAMED 4-step process (Audit→Implement→Automate→Optimize) → gate price behind value → anchor high, discount with scarcity → book next step + take payment ON the call. Paid audit = universal front door + qualifier ("won't pay $300 → won't pay $3k"). Triage call (5 min) disqualifies before wasting 30. Never email a proposal without a call attached.
- **Anti-churn:** build ROI reporting into delivery from day one ("clients care about numbers, not your dashboard"). Monthly one-number impact report.
- **Infra tactics:** 3-layer CLAUDE.md (Directives / Orchestration / Execution + .env); `.env`-guard PreToolUse hook; PostToolUse audit-log hook; `/context` token discipline; convert stable MCP → skill to save tokens; eval-gated skills.
