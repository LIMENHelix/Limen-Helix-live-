## Title
**1099 Energy Deal Broker — Connect Retiring Power Plants to the Developers Who Want Their Grid Slot (Remote, Commission)**

## Job type
Contract · 1099 · Commission-only · Remote · Uncapped

## Compensation
- **[20–35]% of the brokerage / finder fee** on every closed connection you originate.
- Deals are large: a retiring plant's grid interconnection is worth real money to a developer, so a single close is a meaningful check, not a $30 residual.
- **Uncapped.** Build your own book. Top originators graduate to a salaried desk lead and hire brokers under them.
- No base. This is for someone who can work a deal, not clock in.

## What you're brokering (and why it's not obvious money lying around)
When a coal or gas plant retires, most people see scrap. The real asset is the plant's **live grid interconnection** — years of queue time and millions in study costs already paid for. Battery and solar developers desperately want interconnection-ready sites, and the queue to get a new one runs 5+ years.

We hand you a **ranked, national feed of retiring generators** (from federal EIA data), scored by exactly that interconnection value. Your job: reach the plant owner and the repowering developer, and broker the connection. We built the intelligence; you work the relationship and close the finder fee.

## Your day
- We give you the ranked list: which plants are retiring, how big, what fuel, which grid region, how soon.
- You reach the owner (utility / IPP) and the buyers (repowering developers, infrastructure funds, decommissioners — we provide the matched list).
- You broker the introduction and the terms; a licensed principal closes; you keep your cut.

## You'll do well here if you
- Have sold or brokered in energy, power, infrastructure, real estate, or M&A.
- Can get a utility asset manager and a developer on the same call.
- Want a big check per close and your own book, not a salary cap.
- Are comfortable that these are multi-week deals, not one-call closes.

## What we provide
- A ranked national feed of retiring generators scored by interconnection value (no one else packages this for you).
- The matched buyer list per deal.
- Deal desk, templates, and back-office; a licensed principal to close.
- Fast commission on every closed connection.
