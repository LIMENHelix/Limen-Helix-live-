02
Diagnose — the neurology map

    
The mapping turns the domain into a patient. Finance's characteristic lesion is an attention-allocation failure: the anterior-cingulate analogue fires on the loud signal and misses the salient-but-quiet one. That is a specific, nameable pathology, and it points directly at the treatment.

    

      

        
Node / motif

        
dACC · OFC → M6

        
The valuation and conflict-monitoring circuit. Assigns salience, decides what deserves attention.

      

      

        
Function

        
Salience + latency

        
Senses everything, prioritizes nothing in time. Thalamic-gating analogue under sensory flood.

      

      

        
Dysregulation

        
Aberrant salience

        
Loud signals (price, earnings) capture attention; quiet structural distress is missed until it is already priced in.

      

      

        
Indicated treatment

        
A salience prosthetic

        
Restore prioritization: rank the quiet tells above the noise and route them to whoever can act on the lead time.

04
Act — the business it spawns

    
The output of the cycle is not a report, it is a business: Quiet Distress Watch. A ranked, deduplicated feed of filers newly crossing distress thresholds, each with the exact filing excerpt as evidence. It climbs a capital ladder where the no-capital rung deploys first and funds the next.

    

      

        L1
        Per-result distress dossier brokered to one buyer. Own-nothing, EDGAR is public, rendered by the skills above. No audience required.
        buyer:
distressed-debt & claims-trading firms
      

      

        L2
        Scoped alert subscription with kernel score, base-rate, and per-name evidence. Funded by L1.
        buyer:
special-situations desks
      

      

        L3
        Licensed screened feed / data-room against a fund's or litigation-funder's book. Funded by L1 + L2.
        buyer:
funds, litigation finance
