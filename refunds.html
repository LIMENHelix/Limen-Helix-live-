All sales are final

Purchases from LIMEN Helix — subscriptions and one-time orders alike — are final sale. We do not offer refunds, and we do not offer partial refunds for unused time within a paid period.

What you can do instead

Disputes and chargebacks

Nothing on this page limits your card-network rights. If you dispute a charge with your bank, that dispute is handled by your bank and Stripe under their processes, whatever this policy says.
