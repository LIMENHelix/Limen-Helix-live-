What LIMEN Helix is

A cross-domain discovery engine that maps patterns across many fields to surface research leads, treatment hypotheses, operational risks, patentable patterns, grant concepts, and investment theses — and separates proven, unproven, unknown, and impossible states so discoveries can be explored without overstating them.

Why now

Signal is everywhere; meaning is not. Most systems score data inside a single domain. LIMEN Helix maps signals across domains — treating the patterns that break a market, a body, a supply chain, or an institution as variations of the same underlying dynamics — and routes what it finds into research, protection, and execution workflows with an explicit evidence posture attached to every claim.

What it can surface
