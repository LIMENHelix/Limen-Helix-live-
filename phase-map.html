LIMEN HELIX — THE PHASE MAP

P0→P10 recursion grammar · canonical phase arc ↔ financial signature · validated 2026-06
The spine. Every phase is a statement about the recursion operator R — but the operative signal is direction, not synchrony. We tested the Kuramoto order parameter r (phase-coherence across the six lines) two independent ways and it did not separate healthy from distressed companies — a company declining in lockstep is itself highly synchronized (high r), so coherence cannot tell order from collapse. What carries the read is where each of six lines (revenue · gross-margin · profitability · cash · capital · structure) points, read relative to its sector (idiosyncratic, not macro-beta), over time (the walk), with structural events (8-K / 10-12B). The phase is arbitrated from the lines' direction, not from their phase-locking.
Honest scope. The map identifies where a company is and has been across the grammar, idiosyncratically, over time — and explains each break (the driving line that caused it: margin→fracture, capital→scaffold, event→separation). It does not forecast — the driving line moves coincident with the break (~1q lead), not ahead. Validated on known arcs, a blind set, and a 30-company held-out walk (24/30; Wilson 95% CI 62–91%) — retrospective, not yet pre-registered. P9/P10 are defined but not yet detected (rare).
